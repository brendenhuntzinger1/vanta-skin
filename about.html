Our Story

Old-world nourishment, modern skincare.

Vanta Skin started with a simple question: why do so many moisturizers need dozens of ingredients to do one job well?

We went back to basics with grass-fed beef tallow — a rich, skin-compatible fat people have used for generations — and paired it with a few carefully chosen modern ingredients like copper tripeptide-1 (GHK-Cu) and manuka honey. Then we whipped it into a light, airy texture that actually feels good to use every day.

No synthetic fragrance, no filler — just a short list of ingredients that work together.
