Cosmetic products

Vanta Skin products are cosmetics. They are intended to cleanse, moisturize, and improve the appearance of the skin. These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. Our products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Not medical advice

Information on this Site is for general informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a physician or dermatologist before use if you are pregnant or nursing, have a known skin condition, are taking medication, or have known allergies.

Patch test

Before first use, apply a small amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue use if redness, itching, or irritation occurs. If irritation persists, consult a physician.

Allergens

Some products contain animal-derived ingredients (beef tallow, honey, beeswax). Do not use if you are allergic to any listed ingredient. Always review the full ingredient list printed on the product packaging.

Safety

  • For external use only. Do not ingest.
  • Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.
  • Do not apply to broken or irritated skin.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Tallow-based products may soften in heat; this does not affect quality.
