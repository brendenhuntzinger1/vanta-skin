Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to shop with us.

Vanta Skin is committed to making our website accessible to all people, including those with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we're doing

  • Using descriptive alternative text for images
  • Supporting keyboard navigation
  • Maintaining sufficient color contrast
  • Labeling form fields clearly

Need help?

If you have difficulty using any part of our site, please email and we'll be happy to assist you and place your order directly.
